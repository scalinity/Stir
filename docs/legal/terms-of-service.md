## 1. Service description

Stir is an iPhone application that helps you decide what to cook from ingredients you already have. Stir uses artificial intelligence (specifically Google's Gemini family of models) to:

- Identify ingredients from photos of your kitchen
- Suggest dinner ideas based on your ingredients, time, and dietary preferences
- Provide step-by-step cooking guidance with timers
- Suggest substitutions for missing ingredients
- Optionally provide hands-free voice guidance during cooking (Premium subscription required)

## 2. AI-generated content disclaimer

Stir's AI features generate suggestions, instructions, and recommendations based on probabilistic models. These outputs may contain errors, inaccuracies, or omissions. **You are solely responsible for verifying:**

- Food safety and proper handling of all ingredients
- Whether ingredients are safe for any allergies, dietary restrictions, or medical conditions you or your household members may have
- Cooking times and temperatures
- Substitutions suggested by Stir's AI

**Stir is not a substitute for a professional chef, registered dietitian, food safety expert, or medical professional.** Do not rely on Stir for medical, dietary, allergen, or food-safety advice. Always verify ingredient suitability and preparation methods independently.

## 4. Subscription terms

### 4.1 Free tier

Stir offers a free tier that includes:
- 6 Dinner Solves per month
- Unlimited tap-based Cook Mode
- 25 remembered pantry items
- 2 recipe imports per month
- Text-based substitution suggestions

### 4.2 Premium and Pro subscriptions

Premium and Pro subscriptions unlock additional features (voice Cook Mode, more solves, multi-image scans, etc.) on a recurring billing basis:

- **Premium Monthly:** $9.99 USD per month
- **Premium Annual:** $69.99 USD per year (with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers; one trial per Apple ID per subscription group, enforced by Apple)
- **Pro Monthly:** $14.99 USD per month
- **Pro Annual:** $139.99 USD per year

### 4.3 Auto-renewal

Subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Apple charges your iTunes account at confirmation of purchase and at each renewal.

### 4.4 Cancellation

You can cancel anytime through:
- iOS: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Stir > Cancel Subscription
- Mac: System Settings > Apple ID > Media & Purchases > Manage > Stir > Cancel Subscription

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You retain access to paid features until that date.

### 4.5 Trial terms

The 7-day free trial on `stir.premium.annual.trial7` is available once per Apple ID per subscription group, as enforced by the App Store. To avoid being charged after the trial, cancel before the trial ends. Any unused portion of the free trial is forfeited if you purchase the subscription during the trial period.

### 4.6 Refunds

All purchases are handled by Apple and subject to Apple's refund policies. To request a refund, contact Apple directly at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
