# ADR NNNN: <Decision title in one line>

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Deferred | Superseded by NNNN | Rejected
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner-step**: Step N, or "standing" if cross-step
- **Related**: links to spec sections, CLAUDE.md rules, other ADRs, commits, issues

## Context

What's the situation? What forces are in play? Two to four sentences.

## Decision

The choice, stated plainly in one or two sentences. Active voice. Imperative: "Use X," "Forbid Y," "Defer Z until N."

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — one sentence describing it. Rejected because …
- **Option B** — one sentence. Rejected because …

If only one option was viable, say so and give the constraint that made it so.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What gets easier or cheaper.
- What invariant gets protected.

### Negative

- What gets harder.
- What technical debt or future work this creates.

### Tradeoffs

- The price paid and why it's worth it.

## Trigger to revisit (Deferred only)

A concrete, observable signal that reopens this decision. Vague triggers ("if performance becomes a problem") are useless — specify a metric threshold, a release milestone, or an event.

## Notes

Anything else worth keeping: links to cost-model runs, benchmark results, vendor docs, rejected-by-Daniel reasoning. Short-form is fine.
